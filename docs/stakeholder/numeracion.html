Plays numbered by hand

Every play now has FileMaker’s “Configuración del texto”: what is numbered, where the count starts again, and whether the numbers are typed by hand (“Conteo manual”). When the numbers were typed by hand, Playcode keeps them as they are and never counts them again. Sixteen plays have numbers that no count gives, all through the play or from some point on, so they stay typed by hand for now. This page lists them. Question 69 asks whether we may count each one again by the rule shown.

Prepared by Bogdan, 9 November 2026, from the plays Playcode holds.

What counting again would do

Each play would have “Conteo manual” switched off, with the rule shown, and its numbers would be counted again. From then on, every edit counts them again, as in every other play. A number already published, which someone may have cited, would change. The last column says how many would change.

The rules: verses numbers only the verses; verses and prose numbers the prose speeches as well; verses and printed lines numbers the lines of prose as the printed edition breaks them, as 29 English files do; speeches numbers each speech. The count starts again at each scene, at each act, or never (the whole play). In plays that start again, a prologue, an induction, an interlude or an epilogue starts again too, as you told us they do.

Already done, with nothing to ask: 28 plays (and five ARTELOPE plays in our development copy) where only one number was wrong, or where only a prologue or an epilogue went on with the count of the text before it. They are listed under Files corrected, for your own copies. Hamlet (EMOTHE0010, “3-4”, “124.1”) and Timon of Athens (EMOTHE0372, “4-5”) keep their numbers as typed, on purpose. They are not on this page.

El vergonzoso en palacio

In our copy of the file, the verse numbers and the identifiers both go up by threes: em0622-es-0001 is v. 1, -0004 is v. 4, -0007 is v. 7. So the verse numbers seem to have been taken from FileMaker’s count of identifiers, and that count seems to hold two more records per verse that never reach the file. That would explain why “reiniciar la numeración” in FileMaker fixed nothing: it counted the same records again. You corrected the file by hand. Could you send us that copy? We would load it in place of ours, and then this play leaves the list.
