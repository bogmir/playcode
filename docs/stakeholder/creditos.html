Who prepared each digital edition: the TEI files against FileMaker

Each TEI file names the people who prepared its digital edition, and FileMaker keeps the same credits in a table of its own. We compared the two for every play Playcode publishes. They mostly agree. This page lists where they don’t, a mistake of ours we have fixed, and the corrections we plan. The questions it raises are on the project’s list of questions.

Prepared by Bogdan, 11 October 2026, from the TEI files of the published corpus and FileMaker’s credits tables (T07.51), exported on 10 October 2026.

What we compared

FileMaker holds 462 credits, almost all of them “Edición digital”. 413 belong to plays Playcode publishes. Set against the names in each play’s TEI file:

The research groups differ only in how they are written: FileMaker’s “Artelope” against a file’s “Grupo de investigación ARTELOPE, Universitat de València”, or no group in one of the two. Nothing to decide there.

A mistake of ours, now fixed

18 files credit two people in one statement. Playcode read only the first, so the second was missing from the play’s page, the static site and the TEI file it exports. FileMaker credits all of them. Playcode now reads both; they return when the TEI files are next imported.

Corrections made

Where one side is plainly right, Playcode now matches it (question 21, decided on 16 October 2026). A re-import takes the credits from the file again, so the corrections were made in our copies of the files on 18 October 2026; the list of changes for yours is below. Nothing else in the files was changed.

  • “Tronch Pérez, Jesus” becomes “Tronch Pérez, Jesús” in the 63 credits that lack the accent, as FileMaker and his other credits write it.
  • The Revenger’s Tragedy (EMOTHE0278): “Ramírez Sánez, Elena” becomes “Ramírez Sáenz, Elena”, as FileMaker and the three other files that credit her write it.
  • Timon of Athens (EMOTHE0372): the file credits “Editor1” and “Editor2”, as roles “Rol1” and “Rol2”, left over from a template. FileMaker credits Jesús Tronch Pérez, and the file’s own source reads “Edited by Jesús Tronch for the Internet Shakespeare Editions”. We now credit him alone, as the digital editor, the way his other files word it.
  • Marta la Pieuse (EMOTHE0724) and La sortija del olvido (EMOTHE0737): FileMaker’s credit names nobody. We keep the files’ María Teresa Lajoinie Domínguez and Laura Ferrer Blé.
