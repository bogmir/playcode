The form of every play

The revision of question 23: every play in Playcode, 374 of them, with the label its file gives, how much of its text is verse, the form Playcode shows today and the form the rule gives it. Where the rule is wrong for a play, choose its form in the last column; leave the rest as they are.

Prepared by Bogdan, 21 October 2026, from the plays published on Playcode and their TEI files. The question itself: Questions for the project, 23.

The rule

The share of verse is measured by length: the text of the verse lines against the text of the prose, numbered or not; stage directions and headings are not counted. Playcode today calls a play verse when it has one numbered verse line, so it shows verse or prose only; the rule changes the form of at least 74 of the 374 plays. Where the label in the file says otherwise than the rule it is marked verse: 51 plays. A label that counts speeches says nothing either way.
