Draft for the catalogue page. Example data: 11 plays with a few sample passages each. The real page runs on search/records.js and the full-text index.

A. One box, like Google

A single box searches everything at once: title, author, code, character and place of action, and the words of the text and notes. The answer comes in two parts: Obras, the plays whose catalogue record matches (with the reason), and En los textos, the plays whose lines or notes hold the words, with their passages.

The rail goes. The filters sit behind a Filtros button, and each active one shows as a chip you can remove.

Good: nothing to choose before typing. Cost: a play can appear in both parts; the filters are one click further away.

B. Two boxes, filters in a bar

Two boxes side by side, each with one job: Obra for title, author or code, and En el texto for words in the lines and notes. Both combine, and the answer is always one list of plays.

The rail becomes a bar of small menus above the list (Idioma, Tipo, Datación, Personaje…). A menu in use is highlighted; the rarer groups go under Más filtros.

Good: closest to what we have now, and each box says what it does. Cost: two boxes to read before you type; the bar hides the rarer filters.

C. One box that suggests

One box, but it tells you what your word could be. Type sev and it offers: a title with that word, the place of action Sevilla, words in the text, an author, a character. Pick one and it becomes a chip in the box; chips combine. Enter takes the first offer, "title or author".

The rail stays, folded under Más filtros, for the rarer groups.

Good: one box with no guessing about what it searched. Cost: the most new code; an extra step (picking an offer) for someone who only wants to type and press Enter.
